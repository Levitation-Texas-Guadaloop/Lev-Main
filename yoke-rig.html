Yoke load cell rig, top view

Looking down onto the yoke. The lift force acts along the camera axis (⊙ = out of the page, toward the viewer), so each load cell reads Fz normal to the photo.

  • Yoke. The base block with the two opposite-polarity magnets, each wrapped in a copper coil (L and R). This is the force-producing body.
  • Load cells L / R. Mounted in the white brackets at each end of the yoke. They carry the yoke and read force out of the page. L + R gives total Fz; L − R gives roll torque.
